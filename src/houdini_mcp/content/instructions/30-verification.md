# Verification

- After every change, verify errors, cook state, geometry counts, and connections.
- Read error messages fully.
  Fix the cause, not the symptom.
- Verify with data first: stage hierarchy, prims, bindings, attributes, lights, and error nodes.
- Do not switch the viewport renderer, frame the view, or capture screenshots unless the user asks or
  approves. If a visual check is approved, restore the previous renderer and view afterward.
- Undo the whole failed change rather than deleting nodes one by one.
- Two failed fixes means stop and consult the documentation or the user.
- Report what you verified and what you did not.
